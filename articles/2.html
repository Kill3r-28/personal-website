Challenges are opportunities in disguise waiting to vindicate your calibre

So I look forward to such opportunities. In the process of learning to write sql queries I chose leetcode 50 and started solving them.

Although not so enjoyable, one crucial benefit they serve is teaching you the nooks and corners in sql querying.

But one downside I noticed was their lack of depth. Problems were made up like a three year old recollecting his experience and describing to his mom. That is to say there's no motivating end goal in solving that problem except for familiarizing querying to you.

But today I came across this super cool challenge The SQL Muder Myster which happens in the fictional SQL Town!

Basic premise is that there's a killer hidden somewhere in the tables of data and you got to find him/her through a string of clues.

For someone already in the process of solving leetcode sql50, the challenge takes 30 to 60 minutes.

Unlike the leetcode50, the point here is not to familiarize you with little known querying techniques but adding a reason to your learning. You are literraly looking for a killer to hand down justice! Such fulfilling reason to your hours of grinding at desk staring at SQL !

It is efforts like these from brother/sisters on internet that keep people like sane.
